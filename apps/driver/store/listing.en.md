# Querra Driver — store listing (EN)

**Name:** Querra Driver
**Subtitle (iOS, ≤30):** Shifts, trips and CMR for drivers
**Short description (Play, ≤80):** The driver app for transport companies that use Querra Fleet.

**Description:**
Querra Driver is the app for drivers of transport companies that use Querra Fleet. Your account is created by your company; there is no public sign-up.

- Start and end your shift, see assigned trips and confirm loading and unloading.
- Photograph the CMR or proof of delivery and send it straight to your company.
- Turn on GPS only during a shift: your company sees the truck on its map. Private mode and Stop GPS pause collection immediately.
- Get a warning (sound, vibration, banner) when you exceed the speed limit set by your company. The warning helps you notice your speed; it does not guarantee you avoid fines and does not replace road signs.
- Get notifications about trips and documents.

Data is hosted in the EU. GPS positions and speed warnings are deleted automatically after 90 days.
