# App Store — Review notes (EN, paste in App Store Connect)

Querra Driver is a B2B companion app for drivers of haulage companies using Querra Fleet (https://fleet.querra.net). Accounts are created by the company; there is no public sign-up.

Demo account: provide a driver phone + PIN in the "Sign-in required" fields (create a dedicated review driver in a review firm; do NOT reuse a real driver).

Background location (UIBackgroundModes: location): used only after the driver explicitly taps "Activează GPS" during an open shift. It sends the truck position to the driver's own employer for the fleet map and trip confirmations, and shows a local warning when the driver exceeds the speed limit set by the company. The blue location indicator is shown; "Oprește GPS", "Privat" or ending the shift stop collection immediately. No data is used for advertising or tracking.

Speed warnings are informational only: the app does not claim to prevent fines and does not replace road signs.
